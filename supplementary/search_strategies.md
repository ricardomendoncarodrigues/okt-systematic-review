# Search strategies

Databases searched from inception to **3 June 2026**. No date or filter restrictions were applied in the search strings; language eligibility (English, Spanish, French, Portuguese) was applied at screening.

## PubMed (MEDLINE)

```
("orthotopic kidney transplant*"[Title/Abstract] OR "orthotopic renal transplant*"[Title/Abstract] OR "orthotopic kidney graft transplant*"[Title/Abstract] OR "orthotopic renal graft transplant*"[Title/Abstract] OR ("Kidney Transplantation"[Mesh] AND orthotopic[Title/Abstract]))
```

## Scopus

```
TITLE-ABS-KEY("orthotopic kidney transplant" OR "orthotopic renal transplant" OR "orthotopic kidney graft transplant" OR "orthotopic renal graft transplant" OR (orthotopic AND "kidney transplantation"))
```

## Web of Science (Core Collection)

```
TS=("orthotopic kidney transplant" OR "orthotopic renal transplant" OR "orthotopic kidney graft transplant" OR "orthotopic renal graft transplant" OR (orthotopic AND "kidney transplantation"))
```

Reference lists of all included studies and of previously published systematic reviews were also hand-screened (backward citation searching).
